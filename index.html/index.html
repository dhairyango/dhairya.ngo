ABOUT DHAIRYA

Empowering Children Through Hope, Education & Compassion.

Dhairya is a youth-led grassroots organization working with underprivileged children in underserved communities. We engage directly on the ground to understand real challenges and create sustainable, long-term solutions—not just charity.

Our goal is to help children become aware, confident and self-reliant through education, compassion and continuous support while building meaningful relationships with families.

FOUNDER'S MESSAGE

The Heart Behind Dhairya

At just 19, Shantanu is the visionary behind Dhairya, driven by a deep commitment to social change. His journey began at 15, when an idea to bridge the gap between privileged and underprivileged students first inspired him. Though he couldn’t act on it then, the vision stayed with him.With time, determination and support of his friends as well as members of his team he turned that idea into reality by founding Dhairya, guided by the belief “Dhairya se sab hoga”—with patience, everything is possible. Shantanu believes that while one generation may struggle, the next should not have to. Through Dhairya, he is working with his team for the underprivileged children to create opportunities and ensure a better, more hopeful future for those small kids and by also listening to the unheard voices of the society.

"Dhairya Se Sab Hoga."

Shantanu Paul

Founder, Dhairya NGO
